CRM Acquisition & Outreach Pipeline

Mapped CRM bottlenecks responsible for 20–25% lead leakage, then rebuilt the onboarding and outreach pipeline, lifting conversion 20% and cutting cycle time 30%.

← All projects

“Where exactly is the pipeline leaking, and what would it take to close the gap?”

Role
Project Management Consultant
Timeline
Oct – Dec 2025 (3 months)
What I owned
CRM diagnosis, workflow redesign, outreach sequences across 3 accounts

Problem

EuropeLab was losing 20–25% of its leads somewhere between first contact and onboarding, but no one could point to where. The CRM had bottlenecks, the onboarding workflow was inconsistent, and the team wanted to expand into Nordic, medical, and wellness spa channels without first understanding why the existing pipeline leaked.

Process

  • Synthesized findings from 15+ stakeholder interviews and market analysis to locate the exact points where leads were dropping out.
  • Mapped the CRM bottlenecks responsible for the 20–25% leakage and redesigned the onboarding workflow to close the gap.
  • Built a segmented CRM onboarding and nurture pipeline, then wrote and iterated the email and LinkedIn outreach sequences behind it across 3 accounts.
  • Presented market-expansion scenarios for the Nordic, medical, and wellness spa channels, highlighting channel prioritization, feasibility, and risk.

Outcome

  • +20% conversion lift
  • −30% cycle time
  • 20–25% lead leakage identified & closed
  • 15+ stakeholder interviews synthesized

What I learned

A leaking pipeline is rarely one problem; it's several small ones that compound. Interviewing people across the funnel surfaced gaps the data alone didn't show, and fixing the workflow mattered more than adding new outreach on top of a broken one.
