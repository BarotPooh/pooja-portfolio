Hexafort Inc. · Marketing & Strategy

Market Expansion & GTM Strategy

Built a go-to-market strategy for a cybersecurity SaaS entering 5+ global markets, improving targeting precision by 30% and cutting onboarding time by 30%.

← All projects

“Which market is actually worth entering, and how do we reach it without wasting spend?”

Role
Marketing & Strategy Intern
Timeline
May – Aug 2025 (4 months)
What I owned
Market analysis, GTM strategy, performance dashboards

Problem

Hexafort had a cybersecurity solution built for multinational enterprises but no clear way into new markets across the US, Europe, and Asia. Targeting was broad and unsegmented, client onboarding was slow, and each region carried different compliance expectations (SOC 2, ISO, HIPAA) that weren't mapped to the sales motion.

Process

  • Ran cross-border competitive and market analysis across 5+ international markets to find where the product actually had room to win.
  • Built culturally-adapted buyer personas so messaging and targeting matched each region instead of a single generic pitch.
  • Designed performance dashboards from scratch to track segmentation precision and surface where effort was being wasted.
  • Mapped international compliance requirements (SOC 2, ISO, HIPAA) into the onboarding workflow so deals didn't stall on regulatory gaps.

Outcome

  • +30% targeting / segmentation precision
  • −30% client onboarding time
  • 6-figure pipeline growth contributed
  • 5+ markets assessed across US, Europe & Asia

What I learned

The analysis has to come before the outreach. Once segmentation was tight and the personas were real, everything downstream (messaging, onboarding, compliance) got faster and cheaper. Entering fewer, better-fit markets beat spraying effort across all of them.
