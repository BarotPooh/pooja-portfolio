Personal AI Automation

Built automation workflows with n8n and AI tools to remove repetitive reporting work, and built this portfolio site itself as part of that AI-first habit.

← All projects

“What work shouldn't a human be doing by hand anymore?”

Role
Independent · self-directed
Timeline
Ongoing (2025 – present)
What I owned
Workflow design, tool selection, build & iteration

Problem

A lot of marketing and analysis work is repetitive: pulling the same reports, reformatting the same data, writing the same status updates. That time adds up and crowds out the actual thinking. I wanted a standing habit of handing the repetitive parts to AI so I could spend more time on analysis and judgment.

Process

  • Built automation workflows in n8n to remove repetitive reporting and data-handling tasks end to end.
  • Used AI tools day-to-day for market research, competitor analysis, campaign reporting, and drafting, treating them as a thinking partner, not a shortcut.
  • Designed and built this portfolio site itself in collaboration with AI, iterating on structure, copy, and design across many rounds.

Outcome

  • AI-first habit built into daily work
  • Less busywork repetitive reporting automated away
  • This site designed & shipped with AI

What I learned

The win isn't the tool; it's the habit of asking what a human shouldn't be doing by hand. Automating the repetitive layer makes the analytical layer sharper, because there's more time and attention left for it.
