ASU Content Operations

Built and managed a multi-channel content calendar and performance reporting process, improving team output by 25% while keeping publishing on tight deadlines.

← All projects

“Was the content actually landing, and what was slowing the team down?”

Role
Social Media Producer, ASU Enterprise Brand Strategy
Timeline
Dec 2024 – May 2026
What I owned
Content calendar, publishing workflow, performance reporting

Problem

Content was being produced across multiple channels for a large enterprise brand, but without a shared calendar or a clear reporting loop. That meant missed deadlines, inconsistent output, and no reliable read on what was actually resonating with the audience, all while coordinating with Enterprise Brand Strategy, Media Relations, and Strategic Communications teams.

Process

  • Built and managed a multi-channel content calendar so planning, scheduling, and publishing stayed aligned with ASU's brand identity.
  • Set up a performance reporting process to track how content and campaigns performed, turning metrics into decisions instead of guesswork.
  • Partnered across Enterprise Brand Strategy, Media Relations, and Strategic Communications to keep output consistent across platforms.
  • Used AI tools to accelerate reporting and repetitive production work, freeing time for higher-value planning.

Outcome

  • +25% team output
  • +25% audience engagement
  • Multi-channel calendar & reporting, on deadline

What I learned

Most content problems are really operations problems. A shared calendar and a simple reporting loop did more for output and quality than any single piece of content, and made it obvious which work to do more of.
